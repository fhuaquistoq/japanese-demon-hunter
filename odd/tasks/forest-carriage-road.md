# Forest carriage road prototype

## Goal
Turn the Quest 2 carriage prototype into an observable ride: rider initially faces the horses, a lightweight continuous closed rein droops like a rope between both horses and two grab zones, and a recycled forest road with three lanes and stones makes forward motion, braking and smooth lateral shifts visible.

## Accepted decisions
- User selected one closed ring routed from horse to horse via two nearby hand-grab zones. It is one visible continuous rope, not two separate reins or independent handles. Use a bounded gravity/constraint simulation with pinned points to approximate rope physics on Quest 2; do not present it as a chain of rigid-body joints.
- User selected one acceleration gesture after hitting a stone to clear the stop and add speed. Lane changes remain possible while stopped.
- Monsters (now chase from behind, cling and slow carriage; giant kills the player) and the club (2–3 hits to detach) are **future work**, not part of this road milestone. No microphone implementation.
- User alone compiles/builds APK and tests on headset. Assistant must not invoke Unity build, Gradle, player compile, or forced script compilation/refresh. Editor may import new files on its own.

## Architecture and boundaries
- Existing `VehicleRoot` contains `CarriagePrototype`, `OVRCameraRig` (FloorLevel) and rein grab points. World forward is -Z. Rotate only the XR rig's initial facing yaw by 180° relative to the vehicle so camera-forward looks at horses; preserve tracking and body/hand mapping and do not rotate vehicle direction.
- The road/trees/stones live in world space under `RoadRoot`, not under the vehicle. Pool/recycle a bounded set of primitive road tiles/trees as travel advances. Make three lanes visible with lane dividers; procedural arrangements are deterministic, cheap and always leave at least one free lane where obstacles appear. No large collider count or procedural texture assets.
- Preserve the user's existing grabbed-hand gesture gate. Replace the two 2-point tethers with one visibly closed rope, with 2 horse pins and 2 hand-grab pins; segment positions move with gravity/slack, enforce length constraints with reusable arrays (no per-frame allocations), and avoid falling below the deck near the rider. Keep both grabbable zones near reachable rest positions, but make them part of the rope visually. Dropped zones return to rest and the loop remains closed.
- Smooth lane transitions are monotonic; no yaw. Obstacle detection must account for the vehicle's current physical X while changing lanes and sweep its forward Z between frames. On collision speed becomes 0; the struck stone cannot immediately re-trigger on resume. Next valid accelerate gesture clears stop and moves again. Side pulls still work while stopped.

## Acceptance criteria
- On initial Quest view (pending user validation), rider sees horses, road and trees ahead; tracking hands and floor origin still work.
- Road and forest remain in view and recycle in front as vehicle progresses; movement and braking are visually recognizable without chasing a disappearing road segment.
- Single continuous closed rope has visible sag and both horse endpoints stay attached while moving; either or both hand grab zones can move it while held. Gesture commands only come from their matching tracked hands.
- Speed increases on quick lift/down gesture, slows on backward pull, stops at rocks in one or two lanes, and resumes on one accelerate gesture. Lateral steering shifts smoothly among exactly three lanes; edge attempts penalize speed, never leave the road. At least one lane remains unblocked for each obstacle group.
- EditMode tests cover rope closure/pins/slack and lane easing/obstacle one-stroke release/always-open-lane generation. Static Editor hierarchy/serialization check passes; no build/APK by assistant; device observations explicitly remain user-owned.
